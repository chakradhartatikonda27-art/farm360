The challenge

Hand watering and uneven systems waste water and time. A well-planned layout delivers water where plants need it.

Benefits

  • More even watering
  • Less wasted water
  • A system you can operate and maintain
\n

What's included

  • Layout of zones and lines
  • Supply of agreed components
  • Installation and testing
  • A short walkthrough on operating it

Scope, timing and pricing are agreed after assessment.

\n

How it works

  1. 01

    Measure and plan

    We map the planting and water source.

  2. 02

    Install

  3. 03

    Test and explain

    We test coverage and show you how to run it.

Drip suits rows and beds. Sprinklers suit lawns and some field crops. We advise per site.

Can I extend it later?

Yes. We plan zones with expansion in mind.

Is levelling always needed?
